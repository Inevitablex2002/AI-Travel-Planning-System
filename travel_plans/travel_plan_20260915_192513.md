## 6️⃣ PRACTICAL TIPS FOR A SMOOTH TRIP  

1. **Nol Card** – Purchase a **Red Ticket** (pay‑as‑you‑go) at any Metro station. Top‑up ₹500‑₹1,000 for the whole stay.  
2. **Currency** – Use a **no‑foreign‑transaction-fee debit card** (e.g., HDFC, SBI) to withdraw AED at ATMs (≈ ₹22 per AED). Avoid airport currency exchange – it’s pricey.  
3. **Dress Code** – Modest clothing in cultural sites (mosques, souks). Light cotton for daytime, a light jacket for desert evenings.  
4. **Weather** – September‑October: 30‑38 °C, low humidity. Carry sunscreen (SPF 50+), sunglasses, and a reusable water bottle.  
5. **Internet** – Buy a **local SIM** (Etisalat/Du) – 10 GB data for ₹1,200, valid 30 days.  
6. **Safety** – Dubai is very safe, but keep your passport & visa copy in the hotel safe; carry a photocopy when out.
