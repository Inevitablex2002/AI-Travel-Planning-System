## 4️⃣ DAY‑BY‑DAY SIGHTSEEING ITINERARY

- **Morning:** Land at JFK, clear customs, take the **AirTrain + LIRR** to Manhattan (₹2 500).

- **Morning:** **Statue of Liberty & Ellis Island** (ferry + pedestal, ₹6 000).  
- **Afternoon:** **9/11 Memorial & Museum** (₹5 500). Lunch in **West Village** (₹2 500).  
- **Evening:** Broadway show – **“Hamilton”** or similar (₹25 000 for two). Late‑night walk on **High Line**.  

### **Day 3 – Culture & Central Park**
- **Morning:** **Metropolitan Museum of Art** (₹4 500).  
- **Afternoon:** Picnic in **Central Park** (grab bagels & coffee, ₹1 500).  
- **Evening:** Explore **Greenwich Village**, dinner at **Buvette** (₹4 000).  

### **Day 4 – Travel to Washington DC**
- **Morning:** Check‑out, take **Amtrak Northeast Regional** to DC (₹8 000).  
- **Afternoon:** Check‑in at Hotel Harrington, quick lunch at **Ben’s Chili Bowl** (₹1 500).  
- **Evening:** **Monument & Memorial night tour** (free, optional audio guide ₹1 000).  

### **Day 5 – National Mall & Museums**
- **Morning:** **U.S. Capitol Tour** (free, pre‑book).  
- **Afternoon:** **Smithsonian National Air & Space Museum** (free) + **National Museum of Natural History**. Lunch at museum café (₹2 000).  
- **Evening:** Stroll along **Georgetown Waterfront**, dinner at **Fiola** (₹5 000).  

### **Day 6 – More DC + Travel to Chicago**
- **Morning:** Visit **Lincoln Memorial, Reflecting Pool, WWII Memorial**.  
- **Afternoon:** Flight to Chicago (2 h 30 m, ₹14 000).  
- **Evening:** Check‑in at Hyatt Place, dinner at **Giordano’s Deep‑Dish Pizza** (₹2 500).  

### **Day 7 – Chicago Architecture**
- **Morning:** **Architecture River Cruise** (₹5 000).  
- **Afternoon:** Walk **Millennium Park** (Cloud Gate “The Bean”), **Art Institute of Chicago** (₹4 500). Lunch at **The Gage** (₹3 000).  
- **Evening:** **Skydeck at Willis Tower** sunset (₹5 000).  

### **Day 8 – Chicago to Las Vegas**
- **Morning:** Quick visit to **Navy Pier** (cable car, ₹2 000).  
- **Afternoon:** Flight to Las Vegas (₹18 000).  
- **Evening:** Check‑in at The Venetian, explore the **Strip**, dinner at **Jaleo** (₹5 500).  

### **Day 9 – Grand Canyon Day‑Trip**
- **Full Day:** **Grand Canyon West** (incl. Skywalk) via **guided bus tour** – pick‑up from hotel (₹30 000 for two).  
- **Evening:** Return to Vegas, relax at hotel pool.  

### **Day 10 – Las Vegas Highlights**
- **Morning:** **Red Rock Canyon** scenic drive (₹2 500).  
- **Afternoon:** Lunch at **Eataly Las Vegas** (₹2 500).  
- **Evening:** **Cirque du Soleil “O”** show (₹20 000).  

### **Day 11 – Fly to San Francisco**
- **Morning:** Flight to San Francisco (₹15 000).  
- **Afternoon:** Check‑in at Hotel Zephyr, lunch at **Boudin Bakery** (₹2 000).  
- **Evening:** Walk **Pier 39**, watch sea‑lion show, dinner at **Sotto Mare** (₹4 500).  

### **Day 12 – San Francisco Classics**

- **Afternoon:** Check‑out, head to **SFO** via **BART** (₹2 000).
